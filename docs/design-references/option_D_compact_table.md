## 01 &nbsp;&nbsp; Large Text Breaks Bottom Navigation Completely

Navigation tab labels disappear entirely when accessibility text scaling is enabled, leaving users with indistinguishable icons and no way to navigate between app sections.

#### Evidence

> "These buttons down at the bottom — I can't read what they say anymore. I just see little pictures... these three in the middle all look the same to me."
> — PT-003, 00:05:20

> "I've been using this app for two years and I just learned there's a Benefits section."
> — PT-001, 00:07:08

#### Why it matters

Users with accessibility needs cannot access entire sections of the app, creating systematic exclusion from core VA services.

#### Design opportunity

Implement responsive navigation labels that remain visible at all text sizes.
