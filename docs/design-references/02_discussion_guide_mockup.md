## 02 &nbsp;&nbsp; Background and Context

#### Goals

Build rapport, understand the participant's relationship with the VA mobile app, and surface any context that will inform interpretation of later behavior.

#### Questions

1. How long have you been using the VA mobile app?
2. What do you most often use it for?
3. Walk me through the last time you used the app — what were you trying to do?
4. Are there things you wish the app did that it doesn't?
5. Do you use any accessibility features on your phone? Tell me about them.

#### Probes

- Frequency: daily, weekly, monthly, less often?
- When you can't find something in the app, what do you do?
- Have you ever called VA support because the app didn't work? Tell me about that.

## 03 &nbsp;&nbsp; Task 1 — Refill a Prescription

#### Setup

"For our first task, I'd like you to refill a prescription. You don't have to actually submit it — just walk me through what you'd do. Think aloud as you go."

#### Success criteria

- Locates Prescriptions section
- Identifies refill action
- Reaches confirmation screen

#### Observe

- How quickly they locate the section
- What language they use to describe the path
- Any moments of hesitation or backtracking
- Whether assistive technology presents barriers

#### Follow-up questions

1. Was that what you expected?
2. Have you done this before? How often?
3. If you couldn't find it, what would you have done?

---

## 04 &nbsp;&nbsp; Task 2 — Schedule an Appointment

#### Setup

"Now I'd like you to schedule a routine appointment with your primary care doctor. Again, you don't need to actually book it — just go through the steps."

#### Success criteria

- Locates Appointments section
- Reaches calendar view
- Identifies a tappable date
- Reaches time selection

#### Observe

- Whether they find Appointments easily
- How they interact with the calendar
- Any accessibility friction (text size, screen reader announcements)
- Whether they encounter the hidden Continue button

#### Follow-up questions

1. Was the calendar easy to use?
2. Did you see all the buttons you needed?
3. Have you scheduled an appointment in the app before? How did it go?

---

## 05 &nbsp;&nbsp; Task 3 — Check Benefits Status

#### Setup

"Last task — I'd like you to find your current benefits status. Just locate it; you don't need to read the details."

#### Success criteria

- Identifies Benefits section as the destination
- Successfully navigates to Benefits
- Locates status information

#### Observe

- Whether they know Benefits exists in the app
- How they search for it (bottom nav, profile, settings)
- Time to discovery vs. abandonment
- Mental model mismatches (e.g., looking under Profile, Payments)

#### Follow-up questions

1. Did you know Benefits was in the app?
2. What would have made this easier to find?
3. Where did you expect it to be?

---

## 06 &nbsp;&nbsp; Retrospective

#### Goals

Surface overall impressions, frustrations, and unmet needs. Connect to broader app experience beyond today's tasks.

#### Questions

1. Across these three tasks, which felt easiest? Which was hardest?
2. What worked well today?
3. What would you change about the app?
4. If you were describing this app to another veteran, what would you tell them?
5. Is there anything I didn't ask about that I should have?
