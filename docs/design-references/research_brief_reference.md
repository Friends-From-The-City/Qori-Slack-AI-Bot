## Problem

The VA Health & Benefits mobile app's navigation produces a 45% task abandonment rate and 4.2/10 satisfaction score. Support call volume related to "can't find feature" increased 30% quarter-over-quarter. The Q3 2026 redesign is the window to address this — we need evidence-based recommendations on information architecture and navigation patterns before design work begins.

---

## What we'll learn

- Where Veterans expect to find prescriptions, appointments, and benefits information
- How Veterans mentally categorize health vs. benefits content, and where current categorization conflicts with expectations
- Which navigation pattern (bottom tabs vs. hamburger menu) enables faster task completion
- Where Veterans using assistive technology encounter the greatest barriers

---

## Method

**Approach** — Moderated remote usability sessions with task-based scenarios and think-aloud protocol. Sessions will explore navigation pathways for high-frequency tasks (prescriptions, appointments, benefits, messaging, profile).

**Why this method** — Task-based observation lets us see where Veterans get stuck in real time, capturing both behavioral signals (taps, hesitation, backtracking) and attitudinal signals (frustration, confidence). Combined with post-task ratings, we can quantify navigation impact on completion.

---

## Participants

8 Veterans who currently use the VA Health & Benefits mobile app monthly or more frequently. Required composition: 50% using assistive technology, mix of iOS and Android, range of service eras. Recruitment via VA disability services partnerships and Veteran service organizations.
